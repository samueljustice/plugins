# Sound Advice changelog

## 1.1.0

### The book

- It opens as a book. The window shows a closed cover, which swings open onto the entry. Rolling the die or choosing a category shuts the book and opens it again on the new page. The cover is oxblood leather on the paper theme and near-black at night.
- Entries are written in. Each letter's outline is drawn in the accent ink and then fills, and a flourish and the attribution follow.
- Going back turns the page backwards, and switching between paper and night dissolves from one to the other.
- The page has a double-rule frame and a gutter shadow down the spine.

### The entries

- Every entry taken from an article was re-read against its source. 110 were corrected (wrong person credited, a changed fact, a detail the source doesn't give) and 25 were cut because the source didn't support them.
- Every entry taken from a video was re-read against its transcript. 18 were corrected and 2 were cut.
- 30 entries were added from those videos: techniques that had been passed over the first time.
- One credits line was corrected.
