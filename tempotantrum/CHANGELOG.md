# Tempo Tantrum changes

## 0.1.0

The first test build. It is published as a pre-release and is not in the Sammy J's Plugins installer.

It opens a piece of music, finds the hits, maps the beat section by section and puts a bars and beats grid on the hits, with the places it was unsure of listed to check. The map can be corrected by moving, adding and deleting pins, starting a section where the beat restarts and saying which bar a hit is. It plays the music with a click, saves the map beside the music and exports the click as a WAV file. It does not write to Pro Tools yet.
