## Roarschach 1.0.0

The first release. Roarschach turns a voice into a creature: it shifts the pitch and the size of the throat, adds growl, a liquid stereo movement, roar and sub, and finishes with a clean, level output. Two modules can be placed anywhere in the chain: DETAIL, a level compressor that lifts quiet detail and holds back loud parts, and VOCODER, a 32-band vocoder whose carrier follows the voice one or two octaves down.
